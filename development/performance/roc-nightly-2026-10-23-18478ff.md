## Measurement and comparison controls

The baselines were measured with `./scripts/test.py --compare-baselines --jobs 6`
from an empty `XDG_CACHE_HOME`, using the dev backend and Zig 0.16.0 on Linux
x86_64 (x64musl). As in the October 18 review, the arm64mac expectations copy
the x64musl values and were not measured on a Mac.

## Cause of the decreases

The October 18 compiler copied a drawing's command list once for every
`Scene.Drawing.text` or `text_in` call made on a `var` after a loop. The newer
compilers append in place.

`labelled_chart` in `tests/flow_figures/Fixture.roc` builds `var $chart`, runs
a `for` and a `while` loop, then ends with `$chart.text(...)`.
`Scene.Drawing.text` appends to `state.commands`. On October 18 the calls inside
the loops were inlined and appended in place, but the call after the loops went
to a separate procedure that saw `state.commands` as shared. Each such call
allocated and copied a 5,392-byte list. On October 20 and later that call is
inlined too, and the list is unique.

The evidence:

- Recording every `roc_alloc` and `roc_realloc` of `flow figures labels x10`
  under gdb on both compilers and aligning the two sequences leaves exactly
  10 differing events. Each is a fresh 5,392-byte allocation in
  `list_reserve_for_append`, one per figure, and 10 x 5,392 bytes is the
  whole allocated-bytes delta. `faced_chart` ends with two chained post-loop
  calls, `.text_in(Strong, ...).text(...)`, so label faces save two per figure.
- A minimal app that grows a `var` drawing in any loop and then calls `.text`
  allocates 112 times on October 18 and 102 on October 20. Without the loop
  it allocates 62 times on both.
- The decrease is already present in `nightly-2026-10-20-7a8a800` and
  `nightly-2026-10-21-5349569`.

The most likely upstream change is roc-lang/roc#12427 ("Delete pure
definitions orphaned by pruned join parameter writes"), which fixed
roc-lang/roc#12419. Before it, a dead read left behind by a pruned `var`
join-parameter write made ARC treat the value as used later. That forced the
borrowed variant of the call, which copies on append. This was not bisected to
a single commit. The other cases' -2 and -9 deltas were not traced
individually; they plausibly follow the same pattern of calls on a `var` after
a loop.

The rule in [emission linearity](emission-linearity.md) against reassigning an
accumulator in a join is unaffected. This fix removes a copy that the rule did
not cover.

## Compiler regression: cached compile-time literals

The nightly has a regression that blocks a shared compiler cache. When the cache
already holds the package from an earlier root, every later root fails. Each
number literal of a package type with `from_numeral`, such as `Layout.Unit`,
is rejected by compile-time evaluation with "This Roc program overflowed its
stack memory".

- **Scope.** Checking the 13 examples in sequence into one cold cache: the
  first passes and the other 12 fail. Each passes alone or with `--no-cache`,
  and `nightly-2026-10-21-5349569` does not fail.
- **Trigger.** It needs a compile-time-evaluated literal of that type inside
  the package itself, here the `Layout.Unit` expects in `package/Layout.roc`.
- **Cached builds.** A cached `roc build --opt=dev` crashes. In the suite,
  cached fixture builds stop with an unexplained `OutOfMemory`.
- **Upstream.** A five-file standalone reproduction (a local stub platform,
  a one-module package and two apps) was drafted as an upstream issue but not
  yet filed. The likely source is the compile-time object-sharing work merged
  in roc-lang/roc#12399.

**Workaround.** `scripts/test.py` passes `--no-cache` to its `roc check` and
`roc test` validation tasks. Fixture builds already used it. The workaround
carries a TODO naming the issue.

Not affected on this nightly, so left unchanged:

- `scripts/check_gallery.py`, whose `roc run` calls share a cache, reproduces
  all 13 example PDFs.
- `scripts/test_bundle_consumer.py` passes.
- `scripts/fuzz.py` builds all 9 targets into one cache and passes.

Remove the workaround once a nightly checks the examples in sequence into one
cache.

## Toolchain changes

- `roc fmt` reports no formatting changes.
- No new warnings. The `unreachable code` warnings that the automated
  nightly-10-23 update met in the downloaded unicode package are gone: unicode
  4.3.0 no longer reports them, and the harness tolerates warnings located in
  downloaded packages in any case.
