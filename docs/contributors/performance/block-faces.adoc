= Title and heading faces and per-level heading styles

`KernelFacadeShape.style_for` resolves a heading's style from its level.
Semantic planning rejects a level outside 1 to 6 as `UnsupportedHeadingLevel`.

Under an ordered font policy every cluster takes the first policy face that
covers it, and rejects a separately specified title or heading face with
`text.block_font_policy`. A heading whose text its face does not
cover is `text.coverage_missing` located at the heading; no face is
substituted.

The heading styles are fixed-shape records, with no additional list allocation. Candidate lookup scans
at most eight block faces and four role faces per plain block (a constant),
so the styled preparation stays linear in blocks. The faces list is built
once per preparation.

The `Theme` expectation changes one level's style and checks the remaining levels.
