= Selected soft-hyphen

`KernelDiscretionaryHyphen` holds exact scalar and UTF-8 coordinates from the
earlier UAX #14 opportunity. Selection records an independent presentation fact.
The selected soft-hyphen case owns logical `co` + U+00AD + `operate` and paints
a visible hyphen at the selected line end. The external selected-boundary case
paints `a-b` and retains logical `ab`.

The atomic twins reject malformed and unselected external opportunities. The
independent checker inspects exact glyph closure, widths, CID mappings and
balanced replacement text. PDFBox extracts `ab` plus its reader newline even
though the painted U+002D has its own presentation mapping.
